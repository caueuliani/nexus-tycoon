Nexus Tycoon sound effects
==========================

Origin: original procedural synthesis for this project, October 2026.
Source: `tools/generate_sfx.py` (Python standard library; deterministic seed).
License: owned by the Nexus Tycoon project; use and modification for the game,
including commercial distribution, require no third-party attribution.
Third-party material: none. No sounds from other games.

All `sfx_*.wav` files are 16 kHz mono PCM, under 1 second, peak-limited to
0.82 full scale. The app applies a further user-controlled moderate gain.
Files can be replaced independently without changing event routing.
